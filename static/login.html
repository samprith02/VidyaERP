MAWOSfor educational institutions

Multi-Agent Workflow Orchestration System

An ERP for colleges where the office work is done by sixteen specialist agents: timetables and absence cover, leave, gate passes, hostels, buses, the library, placements and certificates. You ask in plain words. The agents prepare the change, and nothing is written until a person approves it.

You ask“Priya is absent on Monday, arrange cover.”
The supervisor routes itTo the agents that own the work.
Agents prepareThree ranked plans, with make-up classes booked.
You approveThe guard holds every change until you say yes.
Written and checkedRe-read after the write, and logged.
RegistrarThe whole institution, the autopilot and the live agent mesh.
Head of departmentThe department's leave, workload and absence cover.
FacultyYour day, your mentees, and leave with its timetable impact worked out.
StudentsAttendance by subject, dues, gate passes, certificates, placements.

This demonstration runs on a synthetic college, Vidyatech Institute of Engineering. No real institution or person is represented.
